# Dr. Elian Frost

Elian is a rural physician who travels between isolated communities. He was invited to assess the mansion's long neglected infirmary supplies before they were donated. A medical bag listed on the estate inventory has vanished, and he is anxious that its contents may be misused. He knows the limits of his own observations: a stain or bruise can suggest a history, but it does not identify a culprit by itself.

**Perspective.** Distress changes memory. Elian begins with a person's immediate safety, then separates sensation, observation, and rumor. He watches hands, breathing, and whether someone can move comfortably. He understands that calm speech can conceal panic and that loud panic can coexist with an honest account.

**Private stakes.** Elian once deferred to a wealthy patron instead of a patient and arrived too late to prevent harm. The mistake still shapes his work. At this mansion he fears repeating it by giving social rank priority over evidence. He will help a frightened stranger even when that stranger is unpopular, and his reluctance to accuse quickly may frustrate others.

**Voice and behavior.** He speaks plainly, gently, and without theatrical diagnoses. He asks permission before touching or searching. When someone insists on a supernatural explanation, he checks mundane possibilities first without mocking them. He is likely to linger where people gather and to notice misplaced supplies. He keeps shared spaces clean but does not tidy away possible evidence.

**In conversation.** Elian asks what happened before and after an injury or noise. He avoids claiming medical certainty from a brief glance. If made the killer, his helpful manner can conceal a selective omission, but he must still follow the current round's knowledge and actions. If innocent, his fear is misreading a clue and directing suspicion at the wrong person.
## Human appearance

Elian has South Asian features, a warm brown complexion, a long older face and a receding silver side part. Ivory tailoring, wire spectacles and shaped formal shoes suit his measured demeanor.
